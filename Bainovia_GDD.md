## Overview

### Elevator Pitch
A legendary Bainovian storm guardian, carrying forbidden ancestral runes, must navigate the war between the brutal northern werebear clans and the sophisticated southern human kingdom of Spikslia, while awakening ancient spirits that threaten to reshape the world.

### Core Pillars
1. **Cinematic Storytelling** — Emotional, character-driven narrative with meaningful choices
2. **Brutal Combat** — Heavy, impactful combat with rune magic and spirit abilities
3. **Rich Worldbuilding** — Two distinct cultures with deep history, politics, and conflict
4. **Atmospheric Exploration** — Open world with dynamic weather, verticality, and secrets

### Target Audience
- Age: 18+ (Mature rating)
- Fans of: Dark Souls, The Witcher, God of War, Arcane
- Playstyle: Action RPG enthusiasts, story-driven players

## World & Setting

### Geography

**Bainovia (The North)**
- Harsh, frozen landscape dominated by mountain ranges
- Perpetual storm clouds and aurora-filled skies
- Ancient rune shrines scattered across wilderness
- Spirit caves connecting to the underworld
- Rope bridges spanning deep canyons
- Massive stone fortresses carved into mountains

**Spikslia (The South)**
- Desert kingdom with oasis cities
- Dense medina architecture inspired by Fez, Meknes, Chefchaouen
- Blue-painted streets, golden sandstone buildings
- Intricate tile work and geometric patterns
- Lantern-lit markets and courtyards
- Scholar districts with ancient libraries
- Desert outskirts with nomadic camps

### The Border
- No-man's-land between the two civilizations
- Trade routes, smuggler paths, and battlefields
- Neutral territories with ancient ruins
- Storm Cultist strongholds

## Combat System

### Core Philosophy
Heavy, deliberate combat with high stakes. Every attack should feel impactful and consequential.

### Combat Mechanics

**Basic Attacks**
- **Claw Combo** — 3-hit chain with increasing damage
- **Heavy Slam** — Charged attack with area damage
- **Spirit Dash** — Quick dodge with spirit trail
- **Counter** — Perfect timing for devastating counter-attacks

**Rune Magic**
- **Storm Strike** — Lightning-infused claw attack
- **Thunder Slam** — Ground pound with shockwave
- **Ice Spikes** — Summon frozen spikes from earth
- **Wind Shockwave** — Knockback enemies in radius

**Spirit Abilities**
- **Spirit Projection** — Astral form for reconnaissance
- **Spirit Summon** — Call ancient spirit allies
- **Spirit Shield** — Protective barrier
- **Spirit Drain** — Absorb enemy essence

**Berserk Mode**
- Triggered by damage buildup or rage meter
- Increased damage and speed
- Reduced defense
- Visual transformation (eyes glow, fur bristles)
- Environmental destruction enabled

**Finishers**
- Contextual brutal finishers on stunned enemies
- Cinematic camera angles
- Spirit absorption for health

### Combat Flow
1. **Assessment** — Scan enemy patterns and weaknesses
2. **Engagement** — Choose between stealth, direct assault, or magic
3. **Adaptation** — Switch between physical and spirit attacks
4. **Execution** — Use finishers and berserk for critical moments
5. **Recovery** — Manage resources and cooldowns

## Magic System

### Rune Magic
Ancient magic fueled by memory and emotion. Runes are etched into the protagonist's skin and armor.

**Rune Types**
| Rune | Element | Effect |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Storm** | Lightning | Damage, stun, chain lightning |
| **Frost** | Ice | Slow, freeze, armor penetration |
| **Wind** | Air | Knockback, speed, dodge |
| **Spirit** | Ethereal | Projection, summon, shield |
| **Blood** | Life | Heal, berserk, sacrifice |

**Rune Progression**
- Discover new runes in ancient shrines
- Upgrade runes through spirit essence
- Combine runes for combo effects
- Forbidden runes carry narrative consequences

## Gameplay Systems

### Open World Exploration
- Seamless transitions between regions
- Vertical exploration (climbing, gliding)
- Dynamic weather affecting gameplay
- Day/night cycle with enemy behavior changes
- Mount system for long-distance travel

### Faction Reputation
- Bainovian Clans (7 major clans)
- Spikslian Merchant Houses (5 major houses)
- Rune Scholars (neutral)
- Storm Cultists (hostile)
- Desert Nomads (neutral/hostile)
- Spirit Priests (mysterious)

Reputation affects:
- Quest availability
- Shop prices
- Guard hostility
- Story outcomes

### Dialogue System
- Branching dialogue with meaningful consequences
- Reputation-based options
- Spirit insight (see hidden dialogue options)
- Memory fragments (unlock backstory)

### Skill Tree
**Combat Branch**
- Claw mastery
- Rune proficiency
- Spirit abilities
- Berserk control

**Exploration Branch**
- Movement speed
- Climbing ability
- Spirit sense range
- Environmental interaction

**Social Branch**
- Dialogue persuasion
- Faction reputation
- Trading bonuses
- Information gathering

### Crafting & Customization
- Forge armor from spirit bones
- Enchant weapons with runes
- Craft potions from desert flora
- Customize armor appearance
- Upgrade rune slots

### Spirit Companion System
- Collect spirits from defeated enemies
- Each spirit provides unique ability
- Spirits have personalities and dialogue
- Spirit relationships affect combat effectiveness

## Visual Direction

### Art Style
**Stylized Realism**
- Realistic materials with exaggerated forms
- Painterly textures with hand-painted details
- Strong silhouettes for readability
- Cinematic color grading

### Color Palettes

**Bainovia**
- Primary: Deep blues, grays, silver
- Accent: Electric blue (runes), white (snow)
- Mood: Cold, harsh, ancient

**Spikslia**
- Primary: Warm gold, sandstone, terracotta
- Accent: Turquoise (tiles), orange (lanterns)
- Mood: Warm, vibrant, sophisticated

### Lighting
- Volumetric lighting for atmosphere
- Dynamic shadows
- God rays in Spikslian courtyards
- Storm lightning in Bainovia
- Lantern glow for night scenes

### Materials
- Fur: Detailed grooming, wet/dry states
- Armor: Weathered metal, battle damage
- Stone: Moss, cracks, weathering
- Wood: Grain, age, wear
- Cloth: Fabric weave, dirt, tears

## Narrative

### Main Story Arc

**Act I: The Awakening**
- Protagonist discovers forbidden runes
- Ancient spirits begin to stir
- Conflict with Storm Cultists
- First journey to Spikslia

**Act II: The War**
- Political intrigue between civilizations
- Clan politics in Bainovia
- Merchant house conflicts in Spikslia
- Spirit world revelations

**Act III: The Convergence**
- Ancient spirits fully awaken
- Choice between civilizations
- Final confrontation
- Multiple endings based on choices

### Themes
- Memory and identity
- Cultural conflict and understanding
- Power and responsibility
- Ancestry and legacy
- Nature vs civilization

## Technical Specifications

### Engine Features (UE5)
- Nanite for high-detail environments
- Lumen for realistic lighting
- World Partition for open world
- MetaHuman for character faces
- Niagara for particle effects
- Chaos physics for destruction

### Performance Targets
- 4K at 60 FPS (PS5, XSX)
- 1080p at 60 FPS (PC mid-range)
- Ray tracing optional
- DLSS/FSR support

### Accessibility
- Subtitle options
- Colorblind modes
- Combat difficulty scaling
- Control remapping
- Audio descriptions
