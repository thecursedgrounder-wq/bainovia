# BAINOVIA — Character Design Document

## Protagonist: Kaelar "Storm-Breaker" Vane

---

## Overview

**Name:** Kaelar Vane  
**Title:** Storm-Breaker (earned during the Spirit Disturbance)  
**Race:** Bainovian (Werebear-humanoid)  
**Age:** 127 years (appears 35 in human form)  
**Gender:** Male  
**Height:** 8.5 feet (transformed), 6.2 feet (human form)  
**Weight:** 450 lbs (transformed), 220 lbs (human form)  
**Voice:** Deep, resonant, speaks slowly and deliberately

---

## Visual Design

### Transformed Form (Werebear)

**Body Type**
- Massive, hunched posture
- Broad shoulders, barrel chest
- Long, powerful arms (40% longer than human proportion)
- Digitigrade legs (reverse-jointed)
- Thick, muscular build
- 7.5-8.5 feet tall

**Fur**
- Primary color: Dark charcoal gray
- Secondary color: Silver streaks on shoulders and back
- Texture: Thick, coarse outer layer, soft underlayer
- Weathered appearance from centuries of battle
- Wet/dry states affect appearance
- Fur bristles during combat or rage

**Face**
- Wolf-like muzzle with powerful jaw
- Human eye placement (forward-facing)
- Eye color: Glowing electric blue (intensifies with power)
- Expressive eyebrows despite fur
- Scars across muzzle and forehead
- Fangs visible even when mouth closed
- Ears: Large, pointed, mobile

**Runes**
- Etched into skin beneath fur
- Glowing electric blue when active
- Patterns: Storm spirals, ancient symbols
- Locations: Forehead, chest, shoulders, forearms, back
- Pulse with energy during combat
- Forbidden runes are darker, more ominous

**Armor**
- Tribal design, minimal but effective
- Made from ancient metal and spirit bones
- Shoulder pauldrons with rune carvings
- Chest plate with ancestral symbols
- Bracers with embedded spirit crystals
- Belt with pouches for runes and supplies
- Fur-lined boots for cold terrain

**Claws**
- Massive, razor-sharp
- Can rend stone and metal
- Glowing tips when charged with energy
- Retractable partially
- Used for climbing and combat

### Human Form

**Appearance**
- 6.2 feet tall, muscular build
- Dark skin with silver scars
- Weathered but handsome face
- Deep-set eyes (still glow faintly)
- Long, dark hair with silver streaks
- Beard with same silver streaks
- Runes visible on skin (less prominent)

**Clothing**
- Simple, practical garments
- Travel-worn cloak
- Leather armor pieces
- Rune amulet around neck
- Heavy boots for terrain

## Personality

### Core Traits
- **Noble:** Strong moral code, protects the innocent
- **Conflicted:** Torn between duty and desire for peace
- **Stoic:** Speaks little, acts decisively
- **Protective:** Fiercely guards those in his care
- **Haunted:** Burdened by memories of the Great Awakening
- **Wise:** Centuries of experience and ancestral knowledge

### Motivations
- Prevent another Great Awakening
- Protect his clan and people
- Understand the forbidden runes
- Find peace between civilizations
- Atone for past mistakes

### Fears
- Losing control to the spirits
- Failing his people
- The ancient spirits fully awakening
- Becoming a monster like the corrupted

### Relationships
- **Clan:** Loyal to the Storm-Clan, respected by others
- **Spirits:** Communicates with ancestral spirits, wary of ancient spirits
- **Spikslia:** Curious but cautious about their culture
- **Storm Cultists:** Hates their fanaticism and danger

---

## Backstory

### Early Life
- Born during the Spirit Disturbance (50 years ago)
- Chosen by spirits at birth
- Marked with first runes as a child
- Trained as Storm Guardian from youth
- Showed exceptional talent for rune magic

### The Awakening
- Discovered forbidden runes in ancient shrine
- First to communicate with ancient spirits
- Warned clan elders of danger
- Exiled temporarily for using forbidden magic
- Returned to save clan from corrupted spirit

### The Storm-Breaker
- Earned title during Spirit Disturbance
- Destroyed corrupted spirit threatening clan
- Absorbed forbidden knowledge
- Burdened with responsibility
- Became guardian of the border

### Current Situation
- Watches over border region
- Studies forbidden runes
- Communicates with spirits
- Prepares for the next awakening
- Caught between civilizations

## Abilities

### Physical Abilities
- **Super Strength:** Can lift 2 tons
- **Enhanced Speed:** 40 mph sprint
- **Enhanced Agility:** Can leap 30 feet
- **Enhanced Senses:** Smell, hearing, night vision
- **Regeneration:** Heals faster than humans
- **Durability:** Resistant to cold and physical damage

### Rune Magic
- **Storm Strike:** Lightning-infused claw attack
- **Thunder Slam:** Ground pound with shockwave
- **Ice Spikes:** Summon frozen spikes from earth
- **Wind Shockwave:** Knockback enemies in radius
- **Spirit Projection:** Astral form for reconnaissance
- **Spirit Summon:** Call ancient spirit allies
- **Spirit Shield:** Protective barrier
- **Spirit Drain:** Absorb enemy essence
- **Rune Explosion:** Detonate runes for area damage
- **Forbidden Runes:** Dangerous, powerful, corrupting

### Combat Style
- **Brutal but calculated:** Heavy attacks with precision
- **Spirit-infused:** Combines physical and magic
- **Environmental:** Uses terrain and weather
- **Adaptive:** Switches between forms and abilities
- **Berserk:** Can enter rage state for power boost

## Voice & Dialogue

### Voice Characteristics
- Deep, resonant bass
- Slow, deliberate speech
- Minimal words, maximum meaning
- Growls when angry
- Whispers when communicating with spirits

### Dialogue Style
- Speaks in short, powerful sentences
- Uses metaphors from nature
- References ancestral wisdom
- Avoids small talk
- Direct and honest

### Sample Lines
- "The storm remembers what we forget."
- "Spirits do not forgive. They remember."
- "The runes speak to those who listen."
- "Strength without wisdom is destruction."
- "I am the storm, and the storm is me."

## Technical Specifications

### Model Requirements
- **Poly Count:** 80,000-120,000 triangles (hero)
- **Texture Resolution:** 4096x4096 for body, 2048x2048 for armor
- **Rig:** Humanoid with additional bones for:
  - Tail (if applicable)
  - Ears (2 bones each)
  - Jaw (for roaring)
  - Individual finger claws
  - Fur simulation bones
- **Blendshapes:** Facial expressions, muscle flexing
- **LODs:** 4 levels (hero, mid, distant, cull)

### Animation States
- Idle (combat/passive)
- Walk/Run
- Sprint
- Jump/Land
- Attack (light/heavy/combo)
- Special abilities (each rune ability)
- Hit reaction
- Death
- Transformation (human ↔ werebear)
- Spirit projection
- Berserk mode

### Material Setup
- **Fur:** Hair shader with groom maps
- **Skin:** Subsurface scattering for ears/nose
- **Armor:** Weathered metal with battle damage
- **Runes:** Emissive material with pulse animation
- **Eyes:** Emissive glow with intensity control
