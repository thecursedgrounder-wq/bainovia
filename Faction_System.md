# BAINOVIA — Faction System Documentation

## Overview

The world of Bainovia is populated by multiple factions, each with their own agendas, beliefs, and relationships with the player. Faction reputation affects quest availability, dialogue options, shop prices, and story outcomes.

## Reputation System

### Reputation Levels

| Level | Name | Effect |
|-------|------|--------|
| -5 | Exiled | Attacked on sight, no services |
| -4 | Hated | Hostile, no trade |
| -3 | Disliked | Unfavorable prices, limited quests |
| -2 | Suspicious | Higher prices, fewer quests |
| -1 | Wary | Normal prices, standard quests |
| 0 | Neutral | Standard prices, standard quests |
| 1 | Trusted | 10% discount, bonus quests |
| 2 | Respected | 20% discount, exclusive quests |
| 3 | Honored | 30% discount, special items |
| 4 | Revered | 40% discount, unique rewards |
| 5 | Legendary | 50% discount, faction leadership |

### Reputation Gains

**Positive Actions**
- Completing faction quests: +5 to +20 reputation
- Defeating faction enemies: +2 to +10 reputation
- Trading with faction: +1 reputation per 100 gold
- Donating to faction: +5 reputation per 50 gold
- Resolving disputes: +10 to +30 reputation
- Discovering faction secrets: +15 reputation

**Negative Actions**
- Attacking faction members: -5 to -20 reputation
- Killing faction leaders: -50 reputation
- Stealing from faction: -10 reputation
- Supporting rival factions: -5 to -15 reputation
- Failing faction quests: -5 to -10 reputation
- Using forbidden runes near faction: -10 reputation

### Reputation Decay
- **Neutral factions:** No decay
- **Allied factions:** -1 reputation per week
- **Hostile factions:** +1 reputation per week (slowly improves)

## Faction Quests

### Quest Types

**Main Faction Quests**
- Advance faction story
- Unlock special abilities
- Reveal faction secrets
- Affect world state

**Side Faction Quests**
- Build reputation
- Earn rewards
- Learn faction lore
- Unlock services

**Emergency Quests**
- Time-sensitive
- High reputation rewards
- Often involve saving faction members

**Reputation Quests**
- Specific to reputation level
- Unlock at certain thresholds
- Provide unique rewards

## Faction Conflict

### War System

**Declaration of War**
- Occurs when reputation reaches -5 with a faction
- Triggers large-scale battles
- Affects world state
- Can be prevented through diplomacy

**War Effects**
- Faction members become hostile
- Trade routes disrupted
- Quest availability changes
- World state permanently altered

**Peace Treaties**
- Can be negotiated through quests
- Requires reputation > 0
- May involve sacrifices
- Restores normal relations

### Civil Wars

**Bainovian Clan Wars**
- Occur between rival clans
- Player can choose sides
- Affects clan relationships
- Can change clan leadership

**Spikslian House Conflicts**
- Political intrigue and trade disputes
- Player can mediate or take sides
- Affects trade and economy
- Can lead to house leadership changes

## Faction Endings

### Bainovian Endings

**Clan Unity**
- All clans united under one banner
- Requires high reputation with all clans
- Bainovia becomes a major power
- Player becomes clan leader

**Clan Independence**
- Clans remain separate but allied
- Requires moderate reputation with all clans
- Bainovia maintains traditional structure
- Player becomes respected advisor

**Clan War**
- Clans fight each other
- Occurs if reputation is low with multiple clans
- Bainovia weakened
- Player must choose a side

### Spikslian Endings

**Merchant Alliance**
- Houses form trade alliance
- Requires high reputation with all houses
- Spikslia becomes economic powerhouse
- Player becomes trade advisor

**Royal Dominance**
- Sultan consolidates power
- Requires high reputation with Al-Medina
- Spikslia becomes centralized
- Player becomes royal advisor

**House Independence**
- Houses remain independent
- Requires moderate reputation with all houses
- Spikslia maintains traditional structure
- Player becomes neutral mediator

## Technical Implementation

### Reputation Tracking
- **Data Structure:** Dictionary of faction IDs to reputation values
- **Persistence:** Saved in player profile
- **Updates:** Real-time reputation changes
- **UI:** Reputation bar in faction menu

### Quest System
- **Quest Database:** Scriptable objects for each quest
- **Faction Assignment:** Quests linked to factions
- **Reputation Gates:** Quests locked until reputation threshold
- **Dynamic Quests:** Procedurally generated side quests

### AI Behavior
- **Reputation-Based:** AI reacts to player reputation
- **Faction Relationships:** AI considers inter-faction relations
- **Dynamic Dialogue:** Dialogue options based on reputation
- **Combat Behavior:** Hostile factions attack on sight
