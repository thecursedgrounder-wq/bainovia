# BAINOVIA — Enemy Bestiary Documentation

## Overview

The world of Bainovia is populated by diverse enemies, from corrupted spirit beasts to rune-mutated creatures. This document details all enemy types, their behaviors, weaknesses, and combat strategies.

---

## Enemy Categories

### 1. Corrupted Spirit Beasts
Animals corrupted by ancient spirits or forbidden magic.

### 2. Rune-Mutated Creatures
Beasts transformed by exposure to rune energy.

### 3. Frost Giants
Massive ice creatures native to Bainovia.

### 4. Sand Wraiths
Desert spirits found in Spikslia.

### 5. Human Mercenaries
Hired soldiers and warriors.

### 6. Ancient Guardians
Magical constructs protecting sacred sites.

### 7. Wild Monsters
Natural creatures of the world.

## Corrupted Spirit Beasts

### Thunder Wolf
**Type:** Corrupted Spirit Beast  
**Region:** Bainovia  
**Health:** 150 HP  
**Damage:** 15-25 per attack  
**Speed:** Fast  
**Behavior:** Pack hunter, uses lightning attacks

**Abilities:**
- **Lightning Bite:** Electrically charged bite (30 damage)
- **Pack Howl:** Buffs nearby wolves
- **Spirit Dash:** Quick movement with spirit trail

**Weaknesses:**
- Frost magic (slows movement)
- Spirit abilities (reveals hidden wolves)

**Loot:**
- Wolf pelt (crafting material)
- Spirit essence (10-20 units)
- Lightning rune shard (rare)

**Strategy:**
- Isolate from pack
- Use frost to slow
- Parry lightning bites
- Finish with spirit drain

## Enemy AI Behavior

### State Machine
1. **Idle:** Waiting for player
2. **Patrol:** Moving through area
3. **Alert:** Detected player
4. **Combat:** Engaged in fight
5. **Stunned:** Vulnerable to finisher
6. **Fleeing:** Running away
7. **Berserk:** Enraged state

### Difficulty Scaling
- **Health:** Scales with player level
- **Damage:** Scales with player level
- **Abilities:** Unlock at higher levels
- **AI:** Smarter at higher difficulties

### Group Behavior
- **Pack Tactics:** Wolves coordinate attacks
- **Formation:** Soldiers use shield walls
- **Support:** Healers and buffers in groups
- **Retreat:** Weaker enemies flee when damaged
