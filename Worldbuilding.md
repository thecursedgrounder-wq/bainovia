# BAINOVIA — Worldbuilding Document

## Overview

The world of Bainovia is divided between two civilizations separated by geography, culture, and ideology. The harsh northern lands of Bainovia and the sophisticated southern kingdom of Spikslia have existed in uneasy peace for centuries, but ancient forces now threaten to reignite conflict.

---

## Bainovia (The North)

### Geography

**Climate**
- Subarctic, perpetually cold
- Storm-filled skies with frequent lightning
- Aurora borealis visible year-round
- Heavy snowfall in winter, freezing rain in summer
- Wind speeds reaching 80 mph in mountain passes

**Terrain**
- **The Frost Peaks:** Massive mountain range dividing the continent
- **The Storm Valley:** Central valley where storms originate
- **The Spirit Caves:** Network of underground tunnels connecting to the spirit realm
- **The Frozen Forest:** Ancient pine forest with spirit-infused trees
- **The Rune Canyons:** Deep canyons with ancient rune carvings on walls
- **The Bone Plains:** Frozen wasteland where ancient battles were fought

**Settlements**
- **Storm-Hold:** Capital fortress carved into Frost Peaks
- **Rune-Shrine:** Ancient temple where runes are studied
- **Clan Villages:** 7 major clan settlements scattered across territory
- **Spirit Wells:** Sacred sites where spirits manifest
- **Border Forts:** Defensive structures along the southern border

### Culture

**The Bainovians**
- Werebear-like humanoids, 7-9 feet tall
- Covered in thick fur (colors: dark gray, black, silver, brown)
- Tribal society organized into clans
- Deep connection to storm magic and spirits
- Value strength, honor, and ancestral wisdom
- Oral tradition, no written language
- Ritualistic approach to magic and combat

**Social Structure**
- **Clan Chiefs:** Leaders of each of 7 major clans
- **Storm Guardians:** Elite warriors with rune magic
- **Spirit Speakers:** Shamans who communicate with spirits
- **Rune Carvers:** Artisans who create and maintain runes
- **Hunters:** Providers and defenders
- **Elders:** Keepers of oral history and tradition

**Beliefs**
- Spirits of ancestors guide and protect
- Storms are manifestations of ancient spirits
- Runes are sacred gifts from the spirit realm
- Death is a return to the spirit world
- Strength is earned through hardship
- Honor is more valuable than life

**Customs**
- **Storm Rites:** Rituals performed during thunderstorms
- **Rune Ceremonies:** Marking of new runes on warriors
- **Spirit Feasts:** Offerings to ancestral spirits
- **Clan Challenges:** Combat to settle disputes
- **Death Rites:** Burning of the fallen to release spirits

### Architecture

**Style**
- Massive stone structures carved into mountains
- Minimal ornamentation, focus on function
- Use of natural materials: stone, bone, fur
- Rune carvings on important buildings
- Rope bridges spanning canyons
- Underground tunnels and chambers

**Key Structures**
- **Storm-Hold:** Capital fortress, largest structure in Bainovia
- **The Great Rune Shrine:** Temple of forbidden knowledge
- **Clan Halls:** Each clan's central building
- **Spirit Wells:** Sacred underground chambers
- **Border Forts:** Defensive towers along southern border

### Flora & Fauna

**Flora**
- **Spirit Pines:** Trees with bioluminescent bark
- **Frost Lilies:** Flowers that bloom only during storms
- **Rune Moss:** Glowing moss that grows on ancient surfaces
- **Ice Vines:** Parasitic plants that freeze their hosts
- **Storm Grass:** Grass that conducts electricity

**Fauna**
- **Thunder Wolves:** Pack hunters with lightning abilities
- **Frost Bears:** Massive bears with ice armor
- **Spirit Hawks:** Birds that can see into spirit realm
- **Stone Crabs:** Armored creatures living in caves
- **Storm Whales:** Ancient creatures in frozen lakes

## Spikslia (The South)

### Geography

**Climate**
- Desert climate with extreme temperature swings
- Hot days (45°C), cold nights (10°C)
- Occasional sandstorms
- Clear skies with intense sunlight
- Oasis regions with microclimates

**Terrain**
- **The Golden Desert:** Vast sand dunes
- **The Oasis Valley:** Fertile region with water sources
- **The Stone Mountains:** Rocky formations with mineral deposits
- **The Salt Flats:** Ancient dried seabed
- **The Coastal Cliffs:** Western coastline

**Settlements**
- **Fez-Al-Madinah:** Capital city, largest in Spikslia
- **Meknes-El-Kebir:** Second city, center of trade
- **Chefchaouen-El-Azraq:** The Blue City, center of scholarship
- **Oasis Towns:** Small settlements around water sources
- **Trading Posts:** Caravan stops along desert routes

### Culture

**The Spikslans**
- Human civilization, North African-inspired
- Sophisticated urban society
- Merchants, scholars, artisans
- Polytheistic religion with spirit worship
- Value knowledge, trade, and diplomacy
- Written language, extensive libraries
- Complex political system

**Social Structure**
- **Sultan:** Ruler of Spikslia
- **Merchant Houses:** 5 major families controlling trade
- **Scholar Guilds:** Academic institutions
- **Military Orders:** Professional soldiers
- **Artisan Guilds:** Craftsmen and builders
- **Common Folk:** Workers, farmers, servants

**Beliefs**
- Multiple gods representing natural forces
- Spirits inhabit sacred places
- Knowledge is the path to enlightenment
- Trade brings prosperity and connection
- Art reflects divine beauty
- Death leads to judgment by the gods

**Customs**
- **Market Days:** Weekly trading festivals
- **Scholar Debates:** Public intellectual discourse
- **Artisan Exhibitions:** Showcases of craftsmanship
- **Religious Festivals:** Celebrations of gods
- **Caravan Departures:** Ceremonial send-offs for traders

### Architecture

**Style**
- Dense medina architecture inspired by Fez, Meknes, Chefchaouen
- Intricate tile work and geometric patterns
- Blue-painted streets and buildings
- Golden sandstone construction
- Courtyard houses with central gardens
- Massive city gates with ornate carvings
- Lantern-lit streets at night
- Rooftop terraces for social gatherings

**Key Structures**
- **The Grand Palace:** Sultan's residence in Fez-Al-Madinah
- **The Great Library:** Repository of knowledge
- **The Blue Mosque:** Center of religious worship
- **The Merchant Exchange:** Hub of trade
- **The Scholar Academy:** Center of learning
- **City Gates:** Massive ornate entrances

### Flora & Fauna

**Flora**
- **Date Palms:** Primary food source
- **Desert Roses:** Flowers with medicinal properties
- **Cacti:** Water storage plants
- **Olive Trees:** Cultivated in oasis regions
- **Sagebrush:** Desert shrub with aromatic leaves

**Fauna**
- **Sand Lions:** Desert predators
- **Camels:** Primary transport animals
- **Desert Hawks:** Birds of prey
- **Scorpions:** Venomous desert creatures
- **Sand Fish:** Creatures that swim through sand

## The Border Region

### Geography
- No-man's-land between Bainovia and Spikslia
- Transition zone from frozen north to desert south
- Rocky terrain with sparse vegetation
- Ancient ruins from forgotten civilization
- Trade routes and smuggler paths
- Storm Cultist strongholds

### Inhabitants
- **Border Hunters:** Independent traders and hunters
- **Storm Cultists:** Fanatics seeking to awaken ancient spirits
- **Desert Nomads:** Wandering tribes
- **Rogue Scholars:** Outcasts from Spikslian guilds
- **Exiled Bainovians:** Those cast out from clans

### Key Locations
- **The Ruined Gate:** Ancient structure of unknown origin
- **The Crossroads:** Meeting point for trade routes
- **The Storm Shrine:** Cultist temple
- **The Oasis of Memory:** Sacred site with ancient knowledge
- **The Bone Pits:** Mass grave from ancient war

## History

### The Great Awakening (500 years ago)
- Ancient spirits first awakened beneath the mountains
- Storms intensified across Bainovia
- First rune carvings appeared
- Spirit communication became possible
- The border region was devastated

### The Clan Wars (300 years ago)
- Bainovian clans fought for control of rune shrines
- Many clans were destroyed or absorbed
- The 7 major clans established
- Storm Guardians emerged as elite warriors
- Spikslia remained neutral but watched

### The Trade Agreement (150 years ago)
- First formal contact between civilizations
- Trade routes established
- Cultural exchange limited but growing
- Border tensions eased
- Scholars began studying Bainovian culture

### The Spirit Disturbance (50 years ago)
- Ancient spirits began to stir again
- Storms intensified in Bainovia
- Storm Cultists emerged in border region
- Spikslian scholars detected anomalies
- The protagonist was born during this time

### The Current Crisis (Present)
- Spirits are awakening faster
- Storm Cultists are gaining power
- Border tensions are rising
- The protagonist discovers forbidden runes
- War threatens to break out

## The Spirit Realm

### Description
- Parallel dimension inhabited by ancestral spirits
- Connected to the physical world through spirit wells
- Time flows differently
- Physical laws are mutable
- Ancient spirits hold forbidden knowledge

### Spirit Types
- **Ancestral Spirits:** Deceased Bainovians
- **Ancient Spirits:** Beings from before recorded history
- **Nature Spirits:** Manifestations of natural forces
- **Storm Spirits:** Beings of lightning and thunder
- **Corrupted Spirits:** Twisted by forbidden magic

### Spirit Interaction
- Spirit Speakers can communicate with spirits
- Runes allow limited spirit projection
- Spirit wells are portals to the spirit realm
- Spirits can possess willing hosts
- Forbidden runes allow dangerous spirit manipulation

---

## Magic System

### Storm Magic
- Native to Bainovia
- Connected to thunder and lightning
- Used by Storm Guardians
- Requires emotional control
- Can be destructive or protective

### Rune Magic
- Ancient, forbidden knowledge
- Etched into skin and armor
- Powered by memory and emotion
- Can summon spirits
- Dangerous if misused

### Spirit Magic
- Communication with ancestral spirits
- Healing and protection
- Divination and prophecy
- Requires training and discipline
- Can corrupt if overused

---

## Technology

### Bainovian Technology
- Primitive but effective
- Stone and bone tools
- Rope bridges and pulleys
- Spirit-infused weapons
- Rune-powered devices
- No written records

### Spikslian Technology
- Advanced for the setting
- Metalworking and engineering
- Written records and libraries
- Trade networks
- Agricultural systems
- Military technology

---

## Languages

### Bainovian
- Oral language only
- Dialects vary by clan
- Complex tonal system
- Used for storytelling and rituals
- No written form

### Spikslian
- Written and spoken
- Multiple dialects
- Used for trade, scholarship, diplomacy
- Extensive literature
- Religious texts

### Trade Tongue
- Simplified language for border trade
- Mix of Bainovian and Spikslian words
- Used by merchants and travelers
- Limited vocabulary
- No written form
