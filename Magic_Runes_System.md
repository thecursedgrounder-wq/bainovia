# BAINOVIA — Magic & Runes System Documentation

## Overview

The magic system of Bainovia is based on ancient rune magic, a forbidden art that draws power from memory, emotion, and the spirit realm. Runes are etched into the protagonist's skin and armor, serving as conduits for elemental and spiritual forces.

---

## Core Philosophy

### Design Principles
1. **Memory-Based Power** — Magic fueled by emotional memories
2. **Visual Storytelling** — Runes glow and pulse with power
3. **Strategic Use** — Cooldowns prevent spam
4. **Combination Potential** — Runes can be chained
5. **Risk/Reward** — Forbidden runes have consequences
6. **Environmental Integration** — Magic interacts with weather and terrain

### Magic Feel
- Runes glow with corresponding element colors
- Spirit energy swirls around the caster
- Audio feedback for each rune activation
- Visual trails and particle effects
- Screen effects on powerful abilities

## Rune Types

### Storm Runes (Lightning)
**Color:** Electric Blue  
**Element:** Lightning, Thunder  
**Emotion:** Anger, Determination  
**Memory:** Storms of the Great Awakening

**Abilities:**
- **Storm Strike:** Lightning-infused claw attack
- **Thunder Slam:** Ground pound with shockwave
- **Chain Lightning:** Arcs between multiple enemies
- **Storm Shield:** Protective lightning barrier

**Visuals:**
- Blue lightning trails
- Thunder clouds forming
- Electric crackling sounds
- Screen flash on impact

---

## Rune Progression

### Discovery
Runes are discovered in ancient shrines and ruins throughout the world:

| Rune Location | Region | Required Quest |
|--------------|--------|----------------|
| Storm Rune | Frost Peaks | "The Lightning Path" |
| Frost Rune | Frozen Forest | "The Ice Shrine" |
| Wind Rune | Rune Canyons | "The Wind Temple" |
| Spirit Rune | Spirit Caves | "The Ancestral Call" |
| Blood Rune | Bone Plains | "The Sacrifice" |
| Forbidden Runes | Ancient Ruins | "The Awakening" |

### Upgrade System
Runes can be upgraded using spirit essence:

**Upgrade Levels:**
- **Level 1:** Basic ability
- **Level 2:** Enhanced damage/range
- **Level 3:** Additional effect
- **Level 4:** Master ability
- **Level 5:** Forbidden power

**Upgrade Costs:**
- Level 1 → 2: 100 spirit essence
- Level 2 → 3: 250 spirit essence
- Level 3 → 4: 500 spirit essence
- Level 4 → 5: 1000 spirit essence

### Rune Slots
The protagonist has limited rune slots on their body:

**Initial Slots:** 3 (forehead, chest, back)  
**Unlockable Slots:** 5 more (shoulders, forearms, legs)  
**Total Slots:** 8

**Slot Unlock:**
- Shoulder slots: Complete "The Storm Guardian" quest
- Forearm slots: Defeat elite enemy
- Leg slots: Reach level 20

## Rune Combinations

### Basic Combos

**Storm + Frost**
- **Effect:** Chain lightning that freezes targets
- **Input:** Storm Strike → Ice Spikes
- **Damage:** 200% total
- **Cooldown:** 15 seconds

**Wind + Thunder**
- **Effect:** Lightning tornado
- **Input:** Wind Shockwave → Thunder Slam
- **Damage:** 250% total
- **Cooldown:** 18 seconds

**Spirit + Blood**
- **Effect:** Spirit drain heals player
- **Input:** Spirit Drain → Blood Sacrifice
- **Healing:** 50% max health
- **Cooldown:** 25 seconds

### Advanced Combos

**Storm + Frost + Wind**
- **Effect:** Freezing lightning storm
- **Input:** Storm Strike → Ice Spikes → Wind Shockwave
- **Damage:** 300% total
- **Cooldown:** 30 seconds

**Spirit + Forbidden**
- **Effect:** Dominate enemy spirit
- **Input:** Spirit Projection → Void Blast
- **Control:** 10 seconds
- **Cooldown:** 60 seconds
- **Risk:** High corruption

**All Five Elements**
- **Effect:** Elemental catastrophe
- **Input:** Chain all five runes in sequence
- **Damage:** 500% total
- **Cooldown:** 90 seconds
- **Risk:** Exhaustion (reduced stats for 30 seconds)

- **Combat Regeneration:** 5 units/kill

## Rune Visual Effects

### Glowing Patterns
Runes etched into skin glow with corresponding element colors:
- **Intensity:** Increases with power level
- **Pulse:** Rhythmic beating like a heart
- **Spread:** Expands during ability use
- **Fade:** Diminishes when depleted

### Particle Effects
Each rune type has unique particle effects:
- **Storm:** Lightning bolts, sparks
- **Frost:** Ice crystals, snowflakes
- **Wind:** Wind distortion, leaves
- **Spirit:** Ethereal wisps, purple glow
- **Blood:** Red mist, droplets
- **Forbidden:** Dark tendrils, void energy

### Environmental Effects
Runes interact with the environment:
- **Storm:** Attracts lightning during storms
- **Frost:** Freezes water and surfaces
- **Wind:** Moves objects and debris
- **Spirit:** Reveals hidden paths
- **Blood:** Stains surfaces temporarily
- **Forbidden:** Corrupts nearby objects

## Technical Considerations

### Performance
- **Particle Effects:** Optimized for 60 FPS
- **Lighting:** Dynamic but performant
- **Audio:** Spatial audio for rune effects
- **UI:** Minimal, unobtrusive

### Implementation
- **Rune System:** Scriptable objects for rune data
- **Cooldown System:** Time-based with modifiers
- **Combo System:** State machine for tracking
- **Visual Effects:** Niagara particle systems

### Balancing
- **Cooldowns:** Prevent spam
- **Damage:** Scaled with level
- **Spirit Essence:** Limited resource
- **Forbidden Runes:** High risk, high reward
