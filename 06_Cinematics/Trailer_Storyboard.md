# BAINOVIA - Trailer Storyboard

**Duration:** 2:30 | **Frames:** 3,600 @ 24fps

---

## FRAME 1: THE PEAK & THE PORTENT (0:00-0:25)

**Scene:** EXT. BAINOVIA MOUNTAINS - NIGHT

**Visual:** High-altitude wide shot positioned at base of jagged cliff face. Frozen mountain peaks pierce through perpetual storm clouds that churn with dark, ominous energy. Lightning crackles across sky, briefly illuminating massive ethereal Bear Spirit silhouetted against clouds. Below, ancient stone archway carved with glowing blue runes pulses rhythmically, marking end of treacherous mountain path. Snow blows horizontally across frame. Deep blues and silvers dominate landscape, rune glow providing only warm light. Aurora borealis dances faintly in upper atmosphere.

**Camera:** WS, Static, slow pan right across range, tilt down to archway

**Audio:** Wind howling through mountain passes, distant thunder rolling, low ominous choir, rhythmic rune pulsing like heartbeat, occasional ice cracking

**Lighting:** Volumetric storm clouds, dynamic lightning, rune glow illuminating snow

**Atmosphere:** Heavy, oppressive, mystical, ancient

**Voiceover:** "The storm remembers what we forget."

---

## FRAME 2: THE DISCOVERY (0:25-0:50)

**Scene:** INT. SPIRIT CAVE

**Visual:** Close-up on Kaelar standing inside hidden shrine deep within mountain. Cave walls are rough-hewn stone covered in ancient carvings and faintly glowing runes that have dimmed over centuries. Kaelar's weathered face shows determination from years of travel and battle. Dark skin etched with simple silver rune scars tracing across forehead, cheeks, and neck - marks of connection to spirit world. Wears worn leather armor testament to long journey. Before him stands massive central stone altar taller than man, carved with intricate darker blue runes that seem to swirl and move when viewed from corner of eye. Hand trembles slightly as he reaches toward forbidden rune at center. Air shimmers with dark energy. Dust motes float in dim blue light emanating from runes. Expression mixes fear, curiosity, and desperate need - knows forbidden but has no choice.

**Camera:** CU, Static, slow push in toward face, rack focus to hand approaching stone

**Audio:** Stone cracking under unseen pressure, energy crackling like static electricity, heavy breathing, intensifying heartbeat drowning out other sounds, faint whispers in ancient language, low hum of altar power

**Lighting:** Single source from glowing runes, casting long shadows, rim lighting on face

**Atmosphere:** Tense, mystical, dangerous, intimate

**Voiceover:** "Spirits do not forgive. They remember."

---

## FRAME 3: THE TRANSFORMATION (0:50-1:15)

**Scene:** INT. SPIRIT CAVE

**Visual:** Dramatic slow motion as Kaelar's fingers make contact with forbidden rune. Moment of contact triggers explosive reaction - dark blue energy erupts from stone, wrapping around arm and spreading rapidly across body. Body begins to contort violently, bones cracking and reshaping with sickening sounds somehow beautiful in terrible power. Dark charcoal fur erupts from skin, growing at impossible speed, covering expanding frame. Muscle mass bulges beneath fur, shoulders widening, chest expanding. Height increases dramatically as legs reshape becoming digitigrade. Face pushes forward into muzzle, teeth elongating into razor-sharp fangs. Blue rune energy arcs off skin like lightning, illuminating entire cave in strobing flashes. Cave walls crack from sheer force of transformation. Kaelar throws head back and lets out roar both human and beast, sound of agony and ecstasy as ancient power floods through him. Eyes glow with intense blue light, then shift to darker predatory hue. Transformation completes in surge of energy blowing dust and debris outward. He stands now as Werebear - eight and a half feet of terrifying power, dark charcoal fur with silver streaks, glowing runes tracing across massive form. Tribal armor materializes around him, forged from spirit energy itself. Cave continues to shake, small stones falling from ceiling.

**Camera:** MS, Action camera, slow motion with subtle shake, circular motion around Kaelar during transformation

**Audio:** Sickening crunch of bones reshaping, tear of muscle and skin, rush of fur growth, energy crackling and arcing like lightning, roar building from human to inhuman, cave groaning and cracking, low-frequency rumble building to crescendo, whoosh of energy displacement

**Lighting:** Strobing blue energy from transformation, dramatic backlighting from altar, particles of energy floating in air

**Atmosphere:** Overwhelming, powerful, terrifying, awe-inspiring

**Voiceover:** "The runes speak to those who listen."

**Title:** BAINOVIA (white flash transition filling screen, then fade to black)

---

## FRAME 4: THE WAR & THE CHOICE (1:15-1:45)

**Scene:** EXT. BORDER BATTLEFIELD

**Visual:** Sweeping wide shot of high mountain pass battlefield where two worlds collide. On left, Bainovian werebears in massive forms charge forward, fur matted with snow and battle scars, wielding spirit-infused weapons that glow with blue energy. They wear tribal armor made from bone and fur, eyes burning with ferocity of northern warriors. On right, Spikslian soldiers in golden sandstone armor advance in disciplined formation, curved blades catching light, banners displaying sun emblem of desert civilization. Two armies meet in center with devastating impact. Dust and snow rise in great clouds, obscuring violence but not hiding brutality. Lightning crackles across battlefield as werebears unleash storm magic, while Spikslian mages counter with fire and light. In exact center stands Kaelar, massive battle-armored Werebear at full height. He does not fight. Stands motionless, caught between two charging forces, massive claws lowered, glowing eyes looking from one army to other. Expression shows profound conflict and heavy burden. Wears intricate tribal armor marking him as something more than warrior - guardian, protector, being caught between worlds. Battlefield stretches for miles behind him, sea of conflict seeming endless. Sky above torn between storm clouds of Bainovia and clear desert sky of Spikslia, as if heavens themselves cannot choose side.

**Camera:** WS, Low angle, subtle shake from battle impact, slow push in toward Kaelar

**Audio:** Deafening roar of two armies colliding, clash of steel on spirit-bone, crackle of storm magic, screams of wounded, thunder of footsteps from thousands of warriors, epic swell of orchestral music building with battle intensity, distant thunder from storm clouds above

**Lighting:** Storm lighting from left, warm desert sun from right, creating dramatic split-lighting effect across battlefield

**Atmosphere:** Epic, tragic, overwhelming, weight of history and inevitable conflict

**Voiceover:** "Two civilizations. One guardian."

---

## FRAME 5: BERSERK & SPIRITS (1:45-2:10)

**Scene:** EXT. BATTLEFIELD

**Visual:** Action-oriented medium shot capturing full ferocity of Kaelar's power unleashed. In corner of frame, Rage Meter UI element flashes red indicating Kaelar has entered Berserk mode. Eyes, previously blue, now glow with intense crimson light burning from within. Fur bristles, standing on end as static electricity crackles across massive form. Grows slightly larger, muscles bulging as ancient battle-rage floods through him. Tears through multiple enemies with brutal devastating efficiency - Spikslian soldier thrown aside like ragdoll, werebear warrior knocked back by single blow. Claws glow with combined red and blue energy, colors swirling together in vortex of destructive power. Around him, ethereal spirit wolves materialize from thin air - ancient spirits, translucent and glowing with blue light, forms shifting between solid and ghostly. Perhaps dozen of them, each unique in appearance but united in purpose. Fight alongside Kaelar, spectral fangs tearing through armor, ghostly forms passing through solid obstacles to reach targets. Battlefield around Kaelar becomes blur of motion - red and blue energy trails follow every movement, spirit wolves dart between enemies, air itself seems to distort from sheer power being unleashed. Debris flies through air, bodies thrown aside, ground cracks from force of impacts. Moves with impossible speed for creature of his size, whirlwind of destruction neither army can withstand. Spirits move in perfect coordination, as if sharing single consciousness, single purpose. Visual contrast between red berserk energy and blue spirit energy creates stunning effect, like fire and ice warring for dominance.

**Camera:** MS, Low angle, aggressive shake, dynamic movement tracking Kaelar through chaos, occasional quick zooms on impact moments

**Audio:** Kaelar's roar deeper and animalistic, heavy metal/industrial musical influence with driving percussion and distorted guitars, wet impact sounds of claws tearing through armor, spectral howl of spirit wolves, crackle of red and blue energy, screams of enemies, thunderous impact of each blow, sound of air being displaced by rapid movement

**Lighting:** Red glow from berserk mode, blue glow from spirits, dynamic shadows from rapid movement, particle effects from energy discharge

**Atmosphere:** Overwhelming, brutal, powerful, unstoppable force

---

## FRAME 6: THE FINALE (2:10-2:30)

**Scene:** EXT. MOUNTAIN PEAK - DAWN

**Visual:** Definitive low-angle wide shot capturing moment of resolution and choice. Sun just beginning to rise over eastern horizon, casting warm golden light across landscape. To west, storm clouds of Bainovia finally fading, dark blues and grays giving way to warm oranges and pinks of dawn. Lighting is transitional - perfect balance between cold of north and warmth of south, as if world itself is healing. Kaelar stands alone on highest mountain peak, Werebear form silhouetted against rising sun. Looks out over valley below where battlefield lies silent now, armies having withdrawn or fallen. Smoke rises from valley floor, testament to violence that has occurred, but also sense of peace settling over land. Kaelar's posture resolute, massive shoulders squared, head held high. Tribal armor gleams in dawn light, runes etched into it pulsing gently, no longer with frantic energy of battle but with steady calm rhythm. Fur, matted with blood and dirt, catches light, turning dark charcoal form into something almost majestic. Spirit wolves that fought alongside him are gone now, returned to spirit realm, their work complete. Kaelar has made choice - not between two civilizations, but third path, path only guardian could walk. Sky above is canvas of transitioning colors, from deep blues of fading night to brilliant golds and oranges of coming day. Clouds drift peacefully now, no longer churned by storm. Peak on which Kaelar stands covered in snow, untouched by battle below, pristine and pure. As camera slowly zooms out, Kaelar becomes smaller against vastness of landscape, single figure standing at edge of world, having witnessed and survived collision of two civilizations. Final image is one of hope, of renewal, of possibility that even after greatest conflicts, peace can be found.

**Camera:** WS, Low angle, extremely slow zoom out revealing more of landscape with each passing second, subtle upward tilt to include more of sky

**Audio:** Epic music reaches climax, full orchestra with choir soaring, then gradually softening as scene progresses, sound of wind across mountain peak (gentle now, not fierce), distant cry of eagle, last echoes of thunder fading into silence, Kaelar's breathing slowing and becoming calm, rustle of snow in dawn breeze, final musical crescendo resolving into single sustained note

**Lighting:** Golden hour lighting from rising sun, soft blue shadows in valleys, rim lighting on Kaelar's silhouette, lens flare from sun

**Atmosphere:** Peaceful, hopeful, resolute, calm after storm, sense of beginning rather than ending

**Title:** BAINOVIA - Coming 2027
