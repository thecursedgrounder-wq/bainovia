## TRAILER NOTES

### Visual Style
- **Bainovia:** Deep blues, grays, silver, electric blue (runes), white (snow)
- **Spikslia:** Warm gold, sandstone, terracotta, turquoise (tiles), orange (lanterns)
- **Lighting:** Volumetric, dynamic shadows, god rays, storm lightning
- **Camera:** Cinematic, dramatic angles, slow motion for impactful moments

### Character Design
- **Kaelar (Human):** 6'2", muscular, dark skin with silver scars, weathered face, glowing eyes
- **Kaelar (Werebear):** 8'5", massive, dark charcoal fur with silver streaks, glowing runes, tribal armor
- **Spirits:** Ethereal, ancient, varied forms (animal, humanoid, abstract)

### Combat Style
- Heavy, deliberate attacks
- Impactful sound design
- Brutal finishers
- Rune-infused combos
- Spirit abilities

### Music Cues
- **Opening:** Atmospheric, Nordic choir, wind, thunder
- **Transformation:** Dramatic, building tension
- **Combat:** Epic orchestral, percussion, heavy metal influence
- **Spikslia:** Middle Eastern instruments, oud, percussion
- **Climax:** Full orchestral, choir, epic crescendo

### Sound Design
- **Transformation:** Bone cracking, energy surge, roar
- **Combat:** Heavy impacts, claw sounds, magic effects
- **Environment:** Wind, thunder, ambient nature sounds
- **Voices:** Deep, resonant for Kaelar; ethereal for spirits

### Marketing Taglines

- "Strength without wisdom is destruction."
